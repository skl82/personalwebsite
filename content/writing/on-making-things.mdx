I have a folder called `ideas/` that is, conservatively, a graveyard. Half-songs.
Paragraphs that never found a second paragraph. A whole app that does one thing
beautifully and nothing else.

For a long time I thought the goal was to *finish*. Lately I think the goal is to
*share the unfinished thing anyway*.

## The demo is the point

A demo isn't a worse version of the song. It's a different artifact — proof that
an idea existed at a moment in time, with all its rough edges intact. Some of my
favorite recordings are demos that someone was brave (or lazy) enough to release.

The same is true of writing. This very post is a draft I decided to stop editing.

## Why it's here

If a thing only counts once it's polished, you'll publish almost nothing. So this
site is, on purpose, a place for half-built things:

1. Songs that are 80% there.
2. Essays that ask a question I haven't answered.
3. Experiments that might break by the time you read this.

Come back in a month. It'll be different. That's the whole idea.
