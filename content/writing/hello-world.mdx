Every few years I get the itch to own a little piece of the web. Not a profile on
someone else's platform, not a feed tuned by a recommendation engine — just a page
that loads when you type my name and shows you what I actually care about.

So here it is.

## What lives here

Three things, mostly:

- **Music** — the stuff I make, and the stuff I can't stop listening to.
- **Writing** — short essays and longer thinking. Some of it starts on
  [Substack](https://substack.com), but the words live here too.
- **Me** — enough to know whether you'd want to grab a coffee.

## A note on the markdown

This post is just a `.mdx` file sitting in a folder. To publish a new one, I drop
in another file. No CMS, no dashboard, no "are you sure you want to publish?"
modal. Friction is the enemy of writing, and this has almost none.

> The best personal site is the one you'll actually update.
